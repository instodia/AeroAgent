OUR CORE USP

Built for Performance. Engineered for Uncompromising Privacy.

Most web agents forward your browsing sessions, sensitive tokens, and DOM contents to third-party cloud servers. AeroAgent is built fundamentally differently.

100% Client-Side Protection

Your browser tokens, cookies, login state, and entered credentials stay confined to your device's secured Chrome/Edge memory. Nothing is stored on our servers.

0% Data shared with external providers

Zero Data Leakage Architecture

All web action scripts, DOM interactions, and form inputs run within an isolated sandboxed context with strict outbound firewall policies.

Strict Sandboxed isolation protocols

You Maintain Full Control

Human-in-the-loop approvals for sensitive clicks, financial actions, or credential fields. AeroAgent pauses and requests one-tap approval before executing critical steps.

1-Click Instant kill switch & step approvals
